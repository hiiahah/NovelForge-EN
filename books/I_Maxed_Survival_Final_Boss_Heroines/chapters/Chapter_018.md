[Lucen Gray’s survival chance has been adjusted from ‘laughable’ to ‘contentious.’]

Not angry.

Old me would have blocked.

Half.

A smile’s dangerous ancestor.

— Scene 2 —

Lucas Ashford had always liked breakfast.

There was something honest about it.

Bread did not pretend to be sword intent.

Eggs did not ask whether he had awakened an ancient bloodline.

Tea sat in the cup and admitted it was tea.

The grand dining hall of Stellaris Royal Academy was already bright when he arrived with Cedric and Mariel, sun pouring through the tall arched windows in clean white bars.

Monday.

Pre-exam day.

The whole hall buzzed with the exact frequency of young nobles trying to sound calm while internally pricing coffins.

Lucas took his tray to their usual table.

Not the center.

Never the center unless duty called him there.

Just close enough that people could find him, far enough that he could breathe.

Cedric dropped into the seat opposite with the exhausted violence of a boy who had been awake since “strategic dawn.”

“If I see one more footwork diagram,” Cedric said, “I will ascend and haunt the library.”

— Scene 3 —

Iris Aurelia did not believe in impossible things.

She believed in classified things.

There was a difference.

Impossible things were for poets, peasants, and people who had never owned a ledger.

Classified things were simply truths with better doors.
