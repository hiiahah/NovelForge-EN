## GLOBAL FRAMEWORK: THE AFFECTION STAGE SYSTEM

Every heroine operates on a concealed **Affection Index** (0–100) that Lucen can only glimpse through indirect System Notes, behavioral cues, and the creeping dread in his gut. The stages are universal, but each heroine expresses them through her unique psychological architecture.

**Stage 0 — NULL / ELIMINATION TARGET (Affection 0–9)**
The heroine registers Lucen as irrelevant background noise, a suspicious anomaly, or an active threat to be removed. Lucen is safest here. He desperately wants to stay here. He cannot.

**Stage 1 — CURIOSITY / FIXATION (Affection 10–29)**
Something Lucen did — always by accident, always while trying to flee — has snagged in the heroine's mind. She begins observing him. Investigating him. Thinking about him at 2 AM when she should be sleeping. Lucen notices her gaze and his survival instincts scream.

**Stage 2 — POSSESSIVE INTEREST / TERRITORIAL MARKING (Affection 30–49)**
The heroine has decided Lucen is *hers* to figure out. She engineers proximity. She finds excuses to touch him, test him, monopolize his schedule. Other heroines entering his orbit triggers visible hostility. Lucen's free time evaporates.

**Stage 3 — OBSESSIVE DEVOTION / EMOTIONAL DEPENDENCY (Affection 50–69)**
Lucen has become the heroine's emotional anchor. His absence causes measurable psychological destabilization. She has memorized his schedule, his habits, the exact way he tilts his head when lying. Her protectiveness becomes terrifying. Faculty begin to notice.

**Stage 4 — CATASTROPHIC LOVE / YANDERE THRESHOLD (Affection 70–89)**
The heroine would restructure civilization for him. Her love has become a geological force. Rejection, perceived abandonment, or the death of Lucen at this stage triggers her Bad End — continental disaster, mass casualty event, or temporal paradox. Lucen is no longer avoiding a date. He is performing active threat containment on a living weapon who wants to hold his hand.

**Stage 5 — TRANSCENDENT BOND / TRUE END LOCK (Affection 90–100)**
Achieved only through Lucen's genuine emotional reciprocation *and* successful navigation of the heroine's deepest trauma. The heroine's destructive potential is not diminished but *redirected* — she becomes his most terrifying ally. Her love stabilizes rather than consumes. This is the only path to the Harem True Ending. Lucen must reach Stage 5 with all four primary heroines simultaneously without any of them killing each other or him.

#### STAGE 4 — CATASTROPHIC LOVE / YANDERE THRESHOLD (Ch 298 → Ch 420)

**Affection Index:** 70–88
**Trigger Event:** The Banquet Confession / Confession Inversion (Ch 298–302).

At the culmination of the Palace Banquet arc (Volume 4), multiple political factions move against Lucen simultaneously, framing him as a spy to force Iris to disavow him. Iris is given a choice by the Imperial Council: denounce the commoner or accept formal censure that weakens her succession claim. She finds Lucen in a palace garden afterward. He is sitting on a bench, staring at the sky, running odds on how many Bad Ends just activated.

"You're safe," she says.

"They were guilty of treason. The timing was convenient."

"Iris."

"The timing," she repeats, quieter, "was convenient."

She sits beside him on the bench. Their shoulders do not touch. The distance between them is approximately four centimeters. It is the most intimate they have ever been. Lucen's System is screaming so many warnings that it has begun abbreviating them: *[BE47 PROX — AFF 71 — CROSS-CONTAM ALERT (CELESTIA 64, RHEA 52, LYRA 47) — HAREM DESTAB WARNING — Evil Goddess: 아 씨발]*

"It's not enough."

**Key Moments (Volumes 4–5):**

**Ch 240 — The Nest.** Lucen discovers that Lyra has been restructuring her dormitory room to resemble a draconic nest — piled with blankets, pillows, warm fabrics, and exactly one Lucen-scented item (a scarf he left behind three weeks ago). The nest is arranged in a perfect spiral, temperature-optimized, and features a Lucen-sized space in the center that she clearly sleeps curled around. She is mortified when he finds it. "It's a draconic instinct thing, it doesn't mean—" "It means you built a nest with a me-shaped hole in it." "...It's a COMFORTABLE me-shaped hole."

**Ch 289 — The Church Discovery.** Church investigators detect Lyra's mana signature. The Dragon Hunt is authorized. Lucen, operating on game knowledge and Iris's political resources (leveraged without Iris fully understanding why), orchestrates a disinformation campaign that reclassifies Lyra's signature as a "dormant artifact" beneath the Academy. Celestia provides a forged Abyssal resonance overlay that masks the draconic frequency. Rhea intimidates the Church's field agent into accepting the cover story through sheer presence. None of the other heroines know Lyra is a dragon. They are simply responding to Lucen's urgent request to protect "a friend." The fact that he can mobilize three of the most dangerous women on the continent to protect a junior cadet they've barely met tells them something about his relationships that they will analyze (jealously, furiously, tenderly) for months.

Lyra learns what Lucen did. She learns that he coordinated a continental deception operation to protect her using nothing but relationships, knowledge, and the desperate improvisation of a man who refuses to let anyone he cares about die.

She does not thank him with words. She crawls through his dormitory window at 2 AM, curls up at the foot of his bed in a ball, and falls asleep. When he wakes and finds her, she says, without opening her eyes: "Hoard-adjacent items sleep near the hoard."

"I thought I *was* the hoard."

"Then the hoard should go back to sleep."

He does. She wraps around his feet and radiates a heat that is just below the discomfort threshold and he sleeps better than he has in months.

**Death Flag Thresholds (Stage 3):**

[Bad End No. 59: The Last Warm Thing] activates if Lucen dies during Stage 3. Unlike The Dragon's Hoard (Stage 2), which is driven by retrieval instinct, The Last Warm Thing is driven by *grief*. #### STAGE 4 — CATASTROPHIC LOVE / YANDERE THRESHOLD (Ch 335 → Ch 435)

**Affection Index:** 70–88
**Trigger Event:** The True Form Flight (Ch 335–340).

During the Calamity Invasions (Volume 5), a Calamity entity threatens to destroy the Academy from above — an aerial assault that no ground-based defense can counter. Lyra transforms. Not one arm. Not a partial shift. Her full true form: a Golden Dragon of blinding radiance, three hundred meters of scaled sovereignty, wings that cast shadows across entire districts. She destroys the entity in a single breath of golden fire and then — instead of reverting — she descends to the Academy courtyard where Lucen is standing, staring up at her with his mouth open.

The entire student body sees her. Faculty. Staff. The remnants of the invasion force. A dragon — a *Golden Dragon*, the apex of all living creatures — lands in the Academy courtyard and lowers her head until one enormous golden eye is level with a boy with 0.009 mana.

She speaks in dragonspeech, which resonates in the bones of everyone present: **"This one is mine."**

Then she reverts to human form and falls into Lucen's arms, unconscious from the mana expenditure.

The political fallout takes seventeen chapters to resolve. Iris handles it.

**Key Moments (Volumes 6–7):**

**Ch 375 — The Time Promise.** Lucen and Lyra have a conversation that breaks them both. She asks: "When you die — when you're old and I'm not — do you want me to stay with you?" "Stay how?" "In human form. Beside you. The whole time." "You'd do that? Spend decades in a form that's too small for you?" "Every form is too small that doesn't have you in it." Lucen: "Then stay. "I'm asking you to carry my name into the future. Because you're the only one who'll remember it in ten thousand years."

She buries her face in his shoulder. She is burning — literally, her skin temperature is dangerous — and he doesn't flinch.

"I'll carry it," she says. "But it's going to hurt."

"I know."

"For forty thousand years."

"I know. I'm sorry."

"Don't be sorry. Be *here*. Right now. That's all I want. Just be here."

He holds her. The stone beneath them glows cherry-red. Neither of them moves.

**Ch 412 — The Hoard Declaration.** During the Church Secrets arc, Lyra formally declares Lucen as her hoard in front of witnesses — an act of draconic law that supersedes all human authority and places him under the protection of dragonkind's most sacred covenant. Violating a dragon's hoard right is the one act that will unite every living dragon in retribution regardless of faction. It is the nuclear option of draconic politics. Lyra deploys it casually, at breakfast, while eating pancakes.

"Lucen is my hoard," she announces to the table, which includes Iris, Celestia, Rhea, and several faculty members.

Dead silence.

"Could you *not* do that over breakfast?" Lucen says.

"The timing felt right."

"The *timing*—"

"Also, this means that hurting him is now an act of war against all dragons. I wanted everyone to know."

She takes a bite of pancake. Iris's eye twitches. Celestia laughs so hard she falls out of her chair. Rhea says "Noted" in a voice that implies she does not consider dragonkind a meaningful obstacle. Faculty begin drafting urgent correspondence.

#### STAGE 5 — TRANSCENDENT BOND / TRUE END LOCK (Ch 435 → Ch 450)

**Affection Index:** 90–100
**Trigger Event:** The Eternal Promise / Starfall Climax (Ch 447–450).

During the Starfall Lament's final convergence, Lyra's role is unique: she must anchor the ritual's temporal component, because only a being that has existed for millennia has the temporal mass to stabilize the time-stream against the extinction event. This means she must be fully present — not in human form, not disguised, but *herself*, in her entirety, visible and vulnerable.

She transforms for the final time during the story. The Golden Dragon rises above the Academy, wings spread, furnace blazing, and Lucen looks up at her and feels the same thing he felt the first time he saw her sleeping in sunlight: *she is the most magnificent thing he has ever seen, and she chose him, and he will spend every remaining day of his life being worthy of that choice.*

The ritual succeeds. The True Ending converges. Lyra descends and reverts and collapses and Lucen catches her (he always catches her) and she says, half-conscious, in a voice that is both human and dragon: "I said your name every night. Did you hear me?"

"Every night," he lies, because he did not hear her, but he knows — in the way that matters — that she said it.

"Lucen Gray," she murmurs, eyes closing.

"Lyra Silverclaw."

"Don't let me forget."

"Never. Not in forty thousand years."

She smiles. Sleeps. Radiates warmth.

**True End Lock Achieved:** Lyra Silverclaw — Affection 100. [Bad End No. 44: Scale and Bone] and [Bad End No. 59: The Last Warm Thing] permanently sealed. The Golden Dragon Shifter becomes the Golden Warden — she does not hide her nature, and the Academy gains the permanent protection of an ancient dragon who has formally declared the institution (and everyone in it) as hoard-by-association. She continues to eat improbable quantities of food. She continues to build nests with Lucen-shaped spaces. She carries the name ledger for the rest of her existence, and in the far future — long after the story ends, long after Lucen Gray has passed from the world — there is a mountain where the air is warm and the wind carries a name, and travelers who rest there say they feel, inexplicably, completely safe.

She remembers. Time tries to take the name. It does not succeed. Not once. Not ever.

The dragon keeps her promise.

### The Jealousy Cascade: Key Cross-Contamination Events

**Event 1 — The Courtyard Kiss Cascade (Ch 89)**
Celestia kisses Lucen → Iris retaliates with schedule manipulation → Rhea increases training frequency → Lyra begins sleeping outside his door. Four heroines, four escalations, one boy considering emigration.

**Event 2 — The Banquet Dance Crisis (Ch 189)**
Iris claims Lucen publicly → Celestia engineers a "coincidental" post-dance garden encounter → Rhea stands against the wall radiating lethal energy → Lyra eats fourteen desserts in visible distress. The System calculates a 73% probability of heroine-on-heroine combat and begins pre-loading Bad End warnings.

**Event 3 — The Medical Ward Convergence (Ch 328)**
After the First Calamity, all four heroines arrive at Lucen's medical ward simultaneously. Iris has jurisdiction (Student Council authority). Celestia has proximity (she was already in the ward treating others). Rhea has legitimacy (she was his combat partner). Lyra has physicality (she simply will not leave). The ward's attending healer later describes the experience as "professionally comparable to treating a patient in the center of a territorial dispute between four natural disasters." Lucen pretends to be asleep. He is not asleep. He is calculating escape routes. There are no escape routes.

**Event 4 — The Harem Negotiation (Ch 420–425)**
The unprecedented event: the four heroines, recognizing the approaching True End scenario, independently reach the same conclusion — Lucen cannot be exclusively claimed without triggering the other three's Bad Ends. A cascading Bad End scenario (one heroine's loss triggering the next's catastrophic response) would destroy the continent four times over. They convene. Without Lucen. Iris chairs. The negotiation takes three chapters and is simultaneously the most politically complex, emotionally volatile, and unintentionally comedic scene in the novel. It ends with a formal accord — the Compact of Four — in which each heroine acknowledges the others' claim and agrees to shared custody of one (1) Lucen Gray, divided by schedule, context, and an elaborate rotation system that Iris designed and Celestia immediately proposed modifications to and Rhea refused to read and Lyra ate (she was nervous; she eats paper when nervous; it is a dragon thing).

The Compact holds. The True Ending becomes possible.
