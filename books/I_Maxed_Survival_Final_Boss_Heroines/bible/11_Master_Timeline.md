### **Pre-Academy Phase (Days −3 to 0)**

---

**DAY −3 (Three Days Before Orientation)**
**「The Death That Wasn't」**

Kang Min-jun, 23-year-old completionist speedrunner (347 hours logged in *Eschaton Hearts*), falls asleep at his desk after clearing his 9th consecutive True Ending variant. He wakes inside the body of **Lucen Gray**, a named-but-disposable NPC whose canonical role is to die during the Tutorial Prologue — crushed by a falling gargoyle during the Academy's Welcome Parade exactly 14 minutes into Chapter 1.

The System boots with an error:

> **[SYSTEM ERROR 0x00000F]**
> Player vessel 「Lucen Gray」 was scheduled for permanent removal at Flag Point 001.
> Removal failed. Reason: UNKNOWN.
> Generating emergency survival parameters...
> **[Status Generated]**
> Name: Lucen Gray (Kang Min-jun)
> Mana Index: 0.009 / 100.000
> STR: F | AGI: F | INT: F | END: F | CHA: F
> Luck: —
> Unique Title: 「A Person Who Should Not Exist」

Lucen finds himself in a cramped boarding-house room in the Lower Capital Ward, three days before Kronhelm Imperial Academy's entrance ceremony. He possesses: one trunk of threadbare uniforms, a half-eaten bread roll, 12 silver coins, and a student admission letter stamped with the lowest priority seal (Gray Tier — charity enrollment for commoner orphans).

He spends the remainder of the day hyperventilating, confirming world details match his game knowledge, and writing the first pages of his **Hangul survival notebook** — a Korean-script encyclopedia of every flag, death trigger, heroine route, and Bad Ending he memorized across 347 hours.

> *'Okay. Okay okay okay. The gargoyle kills "Lucen Gray" in the Welcome Parade because he stands in Row C, Seat 14 of the spectator pavilion. So I just... don't stand there. I don't stand anywhere near there. I stay in bed. I feign illness. I eat this bread roll and I do not go outside.'*

**DAY −2 (Two Days Before Orientation)**
**「Mapping the Kill Zones」**

Lucen scouts the Academy's outer perimeter from a safe distance, confirming the gargoyle's location atop the East Bell Tower. He maps three alternate routes to the registration hall that avoid all known Chapter 1 NPC death flags.

He also confirms a critical detail: **Lucas Ashford**, the game's canonical protagonist (golden-haired, blue-eyed, absurdly handsome swordsman-mage hybrid with S-rank potential), has already arrived at the Capital and is staying in the prestigious Silver Swan Inn.

Lucen's survival rule #1 is established:

> **RULE 1: Never appear on Lucas Ashford's radar. The protagonist's gravity field bends every plot event toward him. Proximity = death flags.**

He purchases cheap supplies from the market district — bandages, a low-grade stamina tonic, two extra bread rolls, ink, and a blank journal to supplement his notebook.

A seemingly trivial encounter: he bumps into a girl in a deep hood buying the same stamina tonic at the apothecary stall. She drops her coin purse. He picks it up and hands it back without looking at her face, mumbles "excuse me," and leaves.

The hooded girl watches him go with curious violet eyes.

> **[Flag Alert — HIDDEN]**
> Contact with 「Unknown Heroine Candidate」 registered.
> Affection cannot be calculated. Encounter logged.

Lucen does not see this alert. The System buries it in a sub-menu he hasn't found yet.

**DAY −1 (One Day Before Orientation)**
**「The Notebook Doctrine」**

Lucen spends the entire day indoors finalizing his survival notebook. He writes in Korean (Hangul) because no writing system in this world resembles it — making the notebook an unbreakable cipher to anyone who finds it.

Key sections drafted:

- **"The 49 Ways I Die"** — An indexed list of every named Bad Ending he can recall, with trigger conditions and avoidance protocols.
- **"Heroine Danger Index"** — Threat assessments for each main heroine ranked by lethality in their Bad End routes.
- **"Lucas Ashford's Key Plot Flags, Chapters 1–100"** — A timeline of the protagonist's major events, so Lucen can always be somewhere else.
- **"The Mob's Creed"** — A personal manifesto on maintaining maximum invisibility.

> *'I am Lucen Gray. Mana Index: 0.009. I am less magically potent than the practice dummies in the training yard. The training dummies have a Mana Index of 0.01. I have confirmed this. The universe has given me less magical potential than an object made of straw and wood. This is fine. This is my advantage. Nobody notices straw.'*

He falls asleep with the notebook clutched to his chest.

**「The Mob Who Didn't Die」**

Lucen executes his survival plan flawlessly. While all Gray Tier students file into the spectator pavilion at Row C, he hides in the maintenance corridor behind the East Hall, listening to the ceremony through the walls.

At exactly 9:07 AM — right on schedule — the ancient gargoyle cracks loose from the East Bell Tower and plummets into the spectator pavilion. Row C, Seat 14 is obliterated. In the game, this kills Lucen Gray and serves as the inciting tragedy that makes Lucas Ashford swear to "protect everyone at this Academy."

Lucen hears the screams. The crash. The chaos.

He sits in the maintenance corridor eating a bread roll.

> **[SYSTEM]**
> Flag Point 001 — 「Gargoyle Death Event」 — Target not present.
> Casualty: 0.
> Narrative deviation detected. Adjusting...
> **[Note: The goddess is mildly annoyed that you refused to die on schedule.]**

**MORNING (9:30–11:00)**

Lucas Ashford heroically shields three students from falling debris despite the gargoyle missing its intended victim. His legend begins on schedule. The narrative self-corrects around the protagonist's heroism.

Lucen emerges from hiding, blends into the panicked crowd, and registers at the administrative office during the confusion. The registrar barely glances at him.

> *Registrar*: "Name?"
> *Lucen*: "Lucen Gray. Gray Tier."
> *Registrar*: "Building D, Room 412. Next."

Building D is the oldest, most decrepit dormitory on campus, reserved for charity-admission commoners. Lucen is delighted. Building D is far from every major plot location.

**AFTERNOON (13:00–15:00)**

The Welcome Assembly in the Grand Auditorium. Department heads introduce themselves. Lucen sits in the very back row, hood partially raised, taking mental notes.

He spots the heroines for the first time in the flesh and nearly chokes on his own saliva:

- **First Princess Iris Aurelia** sits in the royal balcony overlooking the auditorium. Molten-gold eyes sweep the student body like a predator surveying livestock. Her canonical title in the game is 「The Dawn Tyrant」 — she burns the entire Capital to ash in Bad End No. 47: *Sunrise Eternal*. She is the single most dangerous entity in the first four volumes.

- **Alice Rimehart** (true name: Celestia Rimehart, 「The Abyssal Witch」) sits three rows ahead of Lucen, silver hair spilling over her chair. She's humming a nursery rhyme. In Bad End No. 63: *Skin for Love*, she vivisects the protagonist to "understand what makes his heart beat for others and not for her."

- **Rhea Valtor** (「The Ashen Sword」) stands against the wall instead of sitting, arms crossed, grey eyes cold. Rank 1 incoming combat evaluation score. In Bad End No. 29: *The Sword That Cut the Sky*, she bisects a mountain range after losing the person she loves.

Lucen shrinks in his seat.

> *'They're real. They're all real. And they're all beautiful and they will all kill me.'*

**EVENING (18:00–20:00)**

Dormitory assignments finalized. Lucen settles into Room 412 — a broom-closet-sized single with a narrow bed, a desk, and a window overlooking the garbage courtyard. He is overjoyed. No roommate means no witnesses.

He reviews tomorrow's schedule: **Homeroom Class Assignment & Seating Lottery.**

The notebook gains a new entry:

> *'CRITICAL FLAG: The seating lottery. In the game, Lucen Gray is already dead by this point, so his seat is empty. But I'm alive. That means a new seat is generated. If I end up next to a heroine, it's over. I need to rig this somehow.'*

He can't rig it. He has no skills, no connections, and no mana. He goes to sleep in despair.

**DAY 1 — Homeroom & The Catastrophic Seating Lottery**
**「The Empty Seat Beside the Tyrant」**

**MORNING (8:00–10:00)**

Class 1-S (Special Track) homeroom. The Academy sorts students into ranked classes: S, A, B, C, and D. Gray Tier students are normally auto-assigned to Class D. However, due to the gargoyle incident creating administrative chaos, several students were misrouted.

Lucen arrives at what he believes is Class 1-D's homeroom, only to find a marble-floored lecture hall with crystal chandeliers.

> *Lucen*: "...This isn't D."
> *Faculty aide*: "This is 1-S. Are you Lucen Gray? You're on the list. Take your lottery number."
> *Lucen*: "There's been a mistake—"
> *Faculty aide*: "Take. Your. Number."

**THE SEATING LOTTERY:**

Professor Helvetica Stern (Homeroom Advisor, a terrifyingly efficient woman with half-moon spectacles) draws names from an enchanted crystal orb. Seats fill one by one. Lucen watches the heroine seats populate around the room, mentally mapping safe zones.

Row 1 (Front, Center): Lucas Ashford. Iris Aurelia sits two seats away at the end of Row 1 by the window — the royal privilege seat. Row 2 has Rhea Valtor at the aisle. Alice Rimehart is in Row 3, center.

Lucen's name is drawn last.

> *Professor Stern*: "Lucen Gray — Row 1, Seat 2."

Row 1, Seat 2.

Directly between Lucas Ashford (Seat 1) and the empty buffer seat that separates the commoner block from Princess Iris Aurelia's royal-privilege window seat (Seat 3 is the buffer, Iris is Seat 4).

But there's an administrative footnote: the buffer seat was removed this year due to the Empress Consort's new "egalitarian education decree." Seat 3 no longer exists. The row was renumbered.

Lucen Gray is sitting directly beside First Princess Iris Aurelia.

> **[Bad End No. 12: The Tyrant's Curiosity]**
> *Trigger Proximity: CRITICAL.*
> *The Princess notices you. The Princess becomes curious. The Princess does not stop.*
> *Avoidance Protocol: DO NOT BE NOTICED.*

Lucen sits down. He does not breathe. He stares at his desk with the intensity of a man defusing a bomb.

Iris Aurelia glances sideways.

Gold eyes. Analytical. The slightest narrowing.

> *Iris*: "You smell like cheap bread."

> *Lucen*: "...I apologize, Your Highness."

He says nothing else for the rest of homeroom. Iris returns her gaze forward. But her quill, Lucen notices, has stopped writing.

She heard his voice. She registered his existence.

The first crack in the dam.

**DAY 3 — First Academic Lectures & The Borrowed Quill**
**「Invisible Kindness」**

**MORNING (8:00–10:00)**

First official lecture: **Fundamentals of Mana Theory**, taught by Professor Caine Aldric (a wiry man with ink-stained fingers and zero patience).

Lucen sits in his assigned seat beside Iris. He has perfected the art of shrinking. Shoulders drawn, eyes down, breathing shallow. A masterwork of non-presence.

Midway through the lecture, Iris's enchanted quill — a royal-grade artifact that auto-transcribes spoken words — malfunctions. It begins writing gibberish. A tiny disruption, beneath the dignity of a princess to acknowledge publicly, but her note-taking halts.

Lucen notices from his peripheral vision. Without thinking — without making eye contact — he slides his spare (cheap, mundane, non-enchanted) quill across the desk gap toward her, then continues staring at his own notes.

He doesn't say a word.

Iris stares at the crude wooden quill for four seconds. No one has ever offered her something so... unafraid. Courtiers grovel. Nobles bribe. This boy just... slid a quill. Like it was nothing. Like she was just a classmate who needed a pen.

She picks it up. Uses it for the remainder of the lecture. Her handwriting with a mundane quill is unexpectedly clumsy — she's never used one.

After class, she places the quill back on his desk without a word.

Lucen exhales for the first time in ninety minutes.

> *'She didn't execute me. We're fine. We're fine.'*

**EVENING (19:00)**

In her private chambers in the Royal Dormitory (a palace wing unto itself), Iris examines her enchanted quill. It's functioning perfectly now.

She calls her attendant.

> *Iris*: "Eleanor. Have someone look into the student seated beside me. Lucen Gray. Gray Tier admission."
> *Eleanor*: "Yes, Your Highness. What are we looking for?"
> *Iris*: "Everything."

**DAY 5 — Rest Day / Free Period**
**「The Map of Safe Zones」**

Lucen spends his free day mapping every location on campus, classifying them by danger level:

- **RED ZONES** (Heroine Frequent Locations): Royal Garden (Iris), Library Upper Floor (Alice), Training Hall A (Rhea), Dining Hall VIP Section (All).
- **YELLOW ZONES** (Moderate Risk): Main Corridors during class changes, the Auditorium, the Campus Lake.
- **GREEN ZONES** (Safe): Building D, the garbage courtyard, Storage Room B (where the practice dummies live), the maintenance tunnels, the campus chapel basement.

He discovers that **Storage Room B** is essentially abandoned. The practice dummies gather dust. Nobody goes there. It becomes his secret training space — not because he can train effectively with 0.009 mana, but because he can practice the physical evasion techniques he knows from the game's dodge-mechanic tutorials.

> *'I can't fight. I can't cast. But in the game, Lucen Gray's hitbox was small and his i-frames were generous if you knew the timing. Let's see if that translates.'*

He spends three hours practicing rolls, sidesteps, and low-profile movement between the dusty practice dummies.

It's pathetic. But it's something.

**DAY 8 — First Decryption Session**
**「Teaching the Tyrant to Read」**

**EVENING (19:00–21:00)**

Lucen is escorted to the Royal Study Wing by Eleanor (Iris's attendant, a silver-haired woman with the dead eyes of someone who has seen everything). The study room is larger than Lucen's entire dormitory floor.

Iris sits across from him at a mahogany desk. The notebook is open. Tea service is prepared. It is, objectively, the most terrifying tea party in continental history.

Lucen's strategy: teach her a *fake* version of Hangul. Assign false meanings to the characters. He'll claim the notebook is a personal diary about "studying hard and missing his hometown."

> *Iris*: "Begin."
> *Lucen*: "This character here — ㅎ — means 'perseverance.' And this one — ㅏ — means 'under the sky.' So this word reads as 'perseverance under the sky.'"

The actual word is 하 (*ha*), the first syllable of 하렘 (*harem*).

He is teaching the First Princess of the Aurelia Empire that the Korean word for "harem" means "perseverance under the sky."

Iris takes meticulous notes. She is a terrifyingly fast learner.

> *Iris*: "And this passage here? You wrote it multiple times."

She points to a section he recognizes instantly: **"이리스 오렐리아 — 배드 엔딩 No.47: 영원한 일출. 그녀가 당신을 주목하면 도망쳐라."**

(*Iris Aurelia — Bad Ending No. 47: Sunrise Eternal. If she notices you, RUN.*)

> *Lucen*: "That says 'The dawn is most beautiful when witnessed with perseverance. I wrote it as a motivational reminder."
> *Iris*: "..."
> *Iris*: "You wrote my name as a motivational exercise."
> *Lucen*: "Not Your Highness's name specifically—"
> *Iris*: "I can see 'Iris' in your cipher. You pointed out that cluster of symbols corresponds to proper nouns on page three. You're contradicting yourself."

She's catching inconsistencies. In session one.

> *Lucen*: "...It's a passage about how the dawn inspires me. The word for 'dawn' in my cipher coincidentally resembles your name, Your Highness."
> *Iris*: "Coincidentally."
> *Lucen*: "Yes."
> *Iris*: "How fortunate."

Her quill scratches. She writes something in her own journal — in an Imperial cipher that Lucen *can* read because he memorized it during his 347-hour playthrough.

It says: *'He's lying. Continue sessions. Increase frequency to twice weekly.'*

Lucen wants to die. Not in a Bad Ending way. Just in a normal, peaceful, non-princess-related way.

**DAYS 11–14 — Settling Into Routine / Micro-Events**

#### **FINAL ARC — EPILOGUE CHAPTERS (Chapters 531–638)**

The remaining 108 chapters constitute an extended **Harem True Ending Epilogue** — the route that was mathematically impossible in the original game because the player could only choose one heroine.

Lucen, by surviving as a glitch, by refusing to follow any single route, by caring for all four heroines as people rather than "routes," achieved what 347 hours of speedrunning never could: **the True Ending where nobody dies, nobody descends into madness, and the continental extinction is averted by the weakest person in the world.**

Key epilogue threads across Chapters 531–638:

**The Imperial Resolution (Ch. 531–560):** Iris navigates the post-crisis political landscape to reform the Empire. She abolishes the Gray Tier system. Lucen is retroactively reclassified as a "Special Designation Student" — a classification that didn't exist until Iris created it. He attends her coronation as Empress (the Emperor, found alive but abdicated, passes the throne). She doesn't ask him to stand with the court. She asks him to stand beside her. He does.

**The Rimehart Restoration (Ch. 545–575):** Alice/Celestia reclaims her family's name, supported by evidence from the Abyssal Library that proves the Church orchestrated the Rimehart Cataclysm. She establishes a new Rimehart Research Institute dedicated to studying mana anomalies. Lucen is listed as "Permanent Research Subject #1 and Co-Director." She insists the co-director title is purely administrative. It is not.

**The Ashen Sword's Peace (Ch. 555–590):** Rhea, freed from her survivor's guilt by Lucen's rescue during the Starfall, lays down her blade for the first time since childhood. She picks it up again three days later ("I was bored"), but the way she holds it has changed. She fights to protect now, not to atone. She opens a small dojo. Lucen is her only student. He remains terrible. She doesn't care.

**The Dragon's Choice (Ch. 560–600):** Lyra reveals her true nature to the Academy and the Empire. An 800-year-old Golden Dragon attending school and fixating on a human boy is... legally unprecedented. The Imperial Legal Council spends four chapters debating her enrollment status. She resolves the debate by perching on the courthouse roof in full dragon form until they approve her continued enrollment. She completes her education. She plans to stay in human form "for a while." "A while" in dragon terms means several centuries.

**The Protagonist's Friendship (Ch. 580–610):** Lucas Ashford, freed from the game's forced-romance structure, becomes the greatest hero of the age — but also, finally, a person rather than a narrative function. He and Lucen's friendship, forged in genuine mutual respect, becomes the emotional backbone of the post-crisis world. Lucas is the sword. Lucen is the strategy. Together, they're unstoppable. Lucas also, finally, begins his own romance — with a minor character from the Tournament arc (a swordswoman from the Eastern Academy) who likes him for himself, not because the plot demanded it.

**The Notebook's Last Page (Ch. 635–638):** In the final chapters, Lucen retrieves the last section of his Hangul notebook from Iris — the section she held longest, the pages about her route. She's learned to read actual Korean by this point (not his fake system — real Korean, self-taught over three years from context clues and linguistic analysis, because she's *terrifying*). She read the truth long ago. She knows about the Bad Ends. She knows about the game.
